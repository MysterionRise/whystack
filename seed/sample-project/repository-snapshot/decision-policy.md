# Decision Policy

Architecture recommendations are advisory until a named user accepts them. A model may
draft options and assessments, but application code evaluates typed hard constraints and
calculates the displayed weighted score.

A recommendation needs evidence for at least 70 percent of total criterion weight and a
lead of at least five points over the next valid option. Otherwise the system presents a
close call or abstains.

Every externally verifiable claim must cite an immutable source revision and exact
locator. Citation validation permits one repair attempt; a second failure causes
abstention.

Inferred preferences are inactive proposals until a user confirms them. Accepted decisions
and explicitly stated preferences may influence later decisions, and the product must show
which facts affected a ranking.
